## Mastering the Marketplace: Trading Rules and Safety Guidelines

An unregulated player economy invites fraudulent trades, inflated prices, and scam attempts. While the game provides internal trading windows, finalizing arrangements through community channels allows buyers and sellers to negotiate terms, display item screenshots, and organize bulk deliveries smoothly.

Player experience shows that trading etiquette inside the community is strictly enforced. Spamming identical trade requests across multiple channels will quickly earn you a temporary mute from automated moderation bots. Instead, format your offers cleanly using standardized trading acronyms such as WTS (Want to Sell), WTB (Want to Buy), and WTT (Want to Trade).

```text
[WTS] +5 Obsidian Greatsword | Prefix: Savage (+12% Crit) | B/O: 45,000 Gold or Equal Value Runes
[WTB] Pristine Alchemist Flask | Offering: 12,000 Gold | DM Offers Directly
```

To prevent fraudulent transactions, never click unverified links sent via direct messages under the guise of "special game codes" or third-party marketplaces. Always execute the physical item exchange through the safe, built-in player trade window within the game itself.

| Trading Action | Recommended Best Practice | Risky Behavior to Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Price Valuation** | Check historical sales in `#trade-discussion` | Blindly accepting first offer without price comparison |
| **Communication** | Keep initial discussions visible in trade threads | Moving conversations immediately to unmonitored external chats |
| **Item Delivery** | Inspect every trade slot before confirming in-game | Rushing through trades during chaotic local lobby sessions |
| **Middleman Help** | Request verified server staff for high-value swaps | Trusting self-proclaimed "reputation vouches" from random users |

Reviewing general online security resources, such as the [Discord Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines), reinforces best practices for reporting bad actors and maintaining a safe digital environment for everyone in the community.

## Troubleshooting Server Connectivity and Common Verification Errors

Occasionally, technical hitches prevent members from viewing channels or finishing the onboarding process. Discord API slowdowns, bot rate limits, or regional server outages can momentarily stall your verification requests. If you find yourself stuck in a blank server room, taking systematic troubleshooting steps usually resolves the roadblock.

First, check if your Discord client requires an update. Outdated mobile or desktop applications sometimes fail to render interactive verification buttons properly. Second, verify whether your direct messages (DMs) are open to server members, as some security bots deliver instructions via private messages.

| Issue Encountered | Likely Cause | Step-by-Step Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Verification Button Unresponsive** | Bot API lag or temporary timeout | Wait two minutes; restart Discord client and re-click |
| **Cannot Send Messages** | Account is missing the verified role | Complete verification prompts; verify email/phone on Discord |
| **Channels Missing / Empty** | Role synchronization failure | Leave and rejoin, or contact a moderator in `#support-tickets` |
| **Direct Message Scams Received** | Privacy settings set to open | Disable "Allow direct messages from server members" in settings |
| **Accidental Bot Timeout** | Triggered spam filter or link posting | Wait out the timer; read `#server-rules` to avoid repeat infractions |

Maintaining patience during peak launch windows or right after massive content drops will prevent accidental moderation penalties. If persistent errors occur, official staff members are reachable through ticketing systems designed to resolve account synchronization issues without public channel clutter.
